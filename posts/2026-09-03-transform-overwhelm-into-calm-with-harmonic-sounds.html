Integrity is Essential!***

While visiting wellness centers, spas, or therapy environments (including medical offices) where music is featured, please make sure to ask if they maintain an active commercial music license.

Throughout history, music creators have lost significant earnings due to unauthorized public play and the evolution of technology platforms.

Leaning on personal consumer accounts or unlicensed records for business operations directly impacts artists' rightful revenues.

This ongoing injustice toward musicians negatively affects the foundational integrity of healing environments, which we seek to uphold in our spaces. True therapeutic trust cannot exist without ethical practices that honor the art of creation.

By asking one simple question, you uplift the energy of healing spaces everywhere.
