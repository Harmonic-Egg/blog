Discovering Healing Through Music: The Journey of Letting Go

Harmonic Egg Blog / Published October 15, 2026 / 3 min read

There are certain compositions that do more than just introduce sound into our surroundings; they provide an opportunity for introspection, compassion, and the gentle processing of emotions that are often hard to articulate.

This week, we shine a spotlight on Letting Go, a heartfelt 40-minute orchestral piece birthed from the collaboration between Gail Lynn and composer Jeremy Thompson.

This work originated from a request from an ALS foundation located on the East Coast, inviting Gail to create a musical piece tailored for those facing the challenges of amyotrophic lateral sclerosis. The request resonated deeply, igniting both a sense of purpose and responsibility. Reflecting on her journey with the Harmonic Egg®, Gail recognized that music could serve as a tender companion through some of life’s most daunting experiences.

Partnering with Jeremy, who shares a personal connection through the loss of a family member to ALS, they worked together to manifest the vision of Letting Go.

A Soundtrack for Unexpressed Feelings

While Letting Go originated with a specific mission centered on ALS, its emotional reach extends well beyond that particular diagnosis.

Grief can touch us in various forms—from the heartache of losing someone dear to the shifts we undergo with relationships, health, and even life chapters we didn't anticipate. It can also arise as we respond to the changing world around us.

Inspired by the five stages of grief—Denial, Anger, Bargaining, Depression, and Acceptance—this musical composition provides an ambiance conducive to reflection and the release of emotions.

There is no urgency in the journey of processing feelings; rushing could inhibit the healing. Sometimes, simply pausing to listen and connect with the music can be a powerful first step toward finding peace.
