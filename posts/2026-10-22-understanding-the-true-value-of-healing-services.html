Understanding the True Value of Healing Services

Harmonic Egg Blog / Published October 22, 2026 / 1 min read

Let me pose a thought-provoking question:

Would you stroll into a grocery store and announce to the cashier,

"Food should be complimentary since everyone needs to eat?"

I think most of us would agree that this wouldn’t happen.

So why is it common to express that healers should offer their services without charge because healing is essential?

Just because healing is a necessity does not diminish its worth.

Healers, much like anyone else, deserve the opportunity to earn a living from their invaluable work.

When considering the impact of healing on our lives, we can begin to see that healing isn’t merely a service; it embodies a vocation filled with dedication and expertise. If we are willing to pay for nourishment, we should recognize and embrace the cost associated with healing services as well. If you'd like to explore more about why investing in healing is crucial, check out this insightful piece on the topic about the true costs of healing practices.

When we honor the practitioners who facilitate our healing journeys, we uplift the entire wellness community. Every contribution to our well-being is invaluable, and showing that appreciation through fair compensation fosters a healthier society.

Let us embrace the notion that paying for healing is not just a financial exchange; it is an acknowledgment of the transformative power that dedicated healers provide to us all.
